What Lines Up

These toolbox components point at the same kind of component in Figma. Open a card below when the two sides do not match.

  • UiButton, including the icon-only variant, lines up with Buttons
  • UiBreadcrumb lines up with Breadcrumbs
  • UiInput, UiCheckbox, UiSelect, and UiForm line up with Form Inputs
  • UiToggle lines up with Switch
  • UiPagination, UiTable, UiAccordionSection, UiAlert, UiBadge, and UiCard
  • UiText lines up with Typography
  • UiTabs, UiSpinner, and UiDropdownMenu
